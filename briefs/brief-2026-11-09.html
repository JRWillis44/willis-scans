3) The Nexus Analysis

A Three threads now run through one week. First, oil and the Fed: Brent posted its steepest weekly fall since 2020 last week (−16/−17%) on diplomacy hopes, which helps the CPI path but not October's print. If the Qatar track stalls and core CPI prints 0.3%, the market faces higher energy and a near-certain hike together, the same squeeze that drove last Thursday's rout.

A Second, tech as a security asset: the Nvidia story turns export-control enforcement into a counter-intelligence issue. US outlets frame it as national security (AP); Chinese and Singaporean sources frame it as a fabricated smear (Global Times) or a stock-specific sentiment drag (Lianhe Zaobao). Washington's hawks and a Democratic House both gain a reason to tighten rules on Southeast Asian resellers, which would cut against the trade truce Beijing just renewed. Today's chief-of-staff change (Stephen Miller replacing Susie Wiles, per the White House and AP) points to consolidation, not compromise, at home.

A Third, diplomacy before winter: Iran (Doha track), Ukraine (Abu Dhabi Tue–Wed) and China (Shenzhen Nov 18–19) are all live at once. Any one success eases risk premia; the market currently prices partial progress on all three, which leaves room for disappointment.
