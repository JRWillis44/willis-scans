1) Executive Summary (BLUF)

  1. The weekend Doha round lowered the oil risk premium, but neither side has moved on substance. Brent January is down 4.2% at ~$108.6 and WTI is just under $100. We assess a US strike on Iranian territory by Nov 8 is unlikely (~24%, moderate confidence), in line with Polymarket's 23–25%. A strike between Nov 4 and Dec 31 remains likely (70%, moderate), close to Polymarket's ~73% (Kresmion/Polymarket, CoinStats).

The Nasdaq-100 lost more than $2.5T in October. The FT reports OpenAI cut its funding-round target by $10B (Reuters via Investing.com), and Blackstone reportedly told investors Anthropic may be rated high-junk after its IPO (TechCrunch). The US high-yield spread is 340 bp, an ~18-month high (Saxo). We assess it is likely (~60%, moderate) that today's bounce fades before Friday's payrolls rather than marking a durable low. That view changes if Apple (after today's close) and AMD (Tuesday) both beat and guide up.
  3. This is a binary-heavy week: the midterms Tuesday, a possible Iran decision from Wednesday, then the BoE, Norges Bank and payrolls. Polymarket gives Democrats a 73% chance of taking the Senate, and the House is priced as near-certain (Il Sole 24 Ore, Foretell). Options price a ~1.84% S&P move through Nov 6 (Saxo). The quieter shock is in Europe: Poland's court split pushed EUR/PLN to a three-year high (Parkiet). Watch Wednesday and Thursday for NOTAMs or embassy drawdowns (escalation) versus a dated follow-up round (de-escalation) (high confidence these are the diagnostic indicators).
