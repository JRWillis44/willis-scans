  1. Treat Riyadh's confirmation, not Houthi claims, as the oil trigger. If Saudi Arabia or Aramco confirms Petroline damage, expect the pessimistic path to reprice fast (Brent +$8–15). If Riyadh denies it and Tehran yields a joint statement, the premium is likely to compress toward $100–105.

  3. Brazil: separate fiscal from institutional risk. Institutions look intact (4.4M-vote margin, TSE precedent, broad foreign recognition). If the US stays silent and the TSE certifies, an overshoot in BRL and Brazil rates is a later opportunity. If Washington backs annulment, the risk becomes institutional and the premium lasts longer.
