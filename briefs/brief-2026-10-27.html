A The squeeze is migrating from barrels to risk: freight (VLCC ~$1.4M/day), insurance and named infrastructure targets. 8. Strategic Insights

  1. Make Nov 1 the oil hinge, not Nov 3. If Sinokor ships keep transiting after Nov 1 without incident, the lawfare threat is mostly signalling, and premium compression toward $100–105 becomes more likely. A detention or strike would move Brent quickly (+$8–15), because one owner carries ~40% of the barrels.
  2. Trust Riyadh's confirmation over Houthi claims. Saudi Arabia has confirmed airport damage before but not export-asset damage. An Aramco statement or a drop in Kpler Ras Tanura loadings is what to watch. The Saba claim and the X chatter are not.
  3. Fed: watch dissents and December wording. The hold is priced (Kalshi 93%), while December pricing ranges from 75% (Kalshi) to 90% (CME). Whichever venue the statement validates sets the moves in the front end and in rate-sensitive equities.
  4. AI financing: track structure, not just size. Vendor equity (Nvidia into Meta), vendor residual guarantees (Broadcom) and lessor ownership (Apollo/SpaceX) push risk onto suppliers and private credit. Cloud-revenue growth on Wednesday and Thursday decides whether markets treat this as confidence or strain.
  5. Policy analysts: Iran's PGSA is building legal machinery to claim jurisdiction over Hormuz. Expect demands for insurer and flag-state compliance that outlast any ceasefire, a long-term erosion of freedom-of-navigation norms.
