2. Rent roll vs market rent

  • Get the signed rent roll: unit, bed/bath, rent, lease start/end, deposit, who pays which utility, arrears.
  • Compare each unit to market (Zillow ZORI city, Rentometer, 3 live comps within 1 mile, same bed count).
  • Gap = market − in-place. Under-market (>10%) is upside only if leases roll within 12 months and the unit is rent-ready. Over-market (>5%) — underwrite at market, not in-place.
  • Confirm occupancy: ask for 3 months of bank deposits matching the roll.

3. Rent-to-income

  • Area renter median household income (ACS 5-yr B25119, tract or city). Rent ÷ (income ÷ 12) should be ≤ 30%; above 35% = rent ceiling, assume no rent growth.
  • Pull it from data.census.gov (table B25119, renter-occupied median income) for the property's census tract; write it here: $________ → max supportable rent at 30% = $________/mo.

5. Utility setup

  • Separately metered? Electric ___ Gas ___ Water/sewer ___ (Iowa cities bill water/sewer to the owner on many multi-units).
  • Owner-paid utilities from the T-12, per door per month. If owner pays heat (boiler), budget ≥ $90/door/mo and check RUBS feasibility.
  • Trash, snow, lawn, common electric — who pays.

6. Property tax reset

  • Iowa reassesses on sale over time; underwrite taxes at purchase price × county effective rate + 0.10 pt (no homestead credit). Rates used in the scan: Johnson 1.66%, Dubuque 1.36%, Linn 1.76%, Scott 1.62%, Black Hawk 1.63%, Clinton 1.65%, Muscatine 1.65% (source: ACS 2020-24 median taxes ÷ median value via propertytaxrates.org/Tax Foundation; verify the parcel's levy on the county assessor/treasurer site).
  • Mixed-use / commercial-class parcels: check the assessor class; commercial is taxed above the residential rollback.
  • Check for expiring abatements (Urban Revitalization, TIF) — underwrite the full tax from the year it expires.

7. T-12 checks

  • Ask for the trailing-12 P&L and the 2 prior years' Schedule E.
  • Reconcile T-12 income to the rent roll (gap > 5% = vacancy or concessions not disclosed).
  • Expense ratio: below 40% on a building with owner-paid utilities is a red flag (missing items). Card assumption is 52% of EGI.
  • Line items to verify: taxes (bill), insurance (quote your own), utilities (12 months of bills), repairs, management (use 8–10% even if self-managed), turnover cost, legal/evictions.

8. DSCR and break-even

  • DSCR = NOI ÷ annual P&I. Annual P&I = 0.05689 × price (2–8: 75% loan, 6.50%, 30 yr) or 0.06077 × price (8–24: 7.15%). Lenders want ≥ 1.25.
  • Break-even occupancy = (operating expenses excl. vacancy + P&I) ÷ GPR. < 80% safe · 80–88% watch · > 88% fragile.

9. Returns

  • CoC after reserves = (NOI − reserves − P&I) ÷ (28% cash-in + rehab).
  • 10-yr IRR (conservative): appreciation at the county's 2005–15 pace, NOI +1.5%/yr (+1.0% in C), 6% sale cost.
  • Equity multiple = (all cash flow + net sale proceeds) ÷ cash invested. Target ≥ 2.0×.
  • Exit cap stress: re-run the 10-yr with the sale at going-in cap + 1.0 pt. Below 8%/yr = APPRECIATION-DEPENDENT — only proceed if CoC carries the deal.

10. Tenant concentration (8–24, mixed-use)

  • Any single tenant > 15% of rent (20% in A) → get their lease, estoppel and payment history. Commercial ground-floor tenants: lease term ≥ 3 yrs remaining or underwrite vacancy.
  • Section 8 / HAP share: fine, but confirm inspections are current.
